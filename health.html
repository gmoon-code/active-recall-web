How to check offline access

Open the main app once while online. After it loads, turn off internet access, then reload it. The app should still open and show your saved information. Reconnect afterward. Cache readiness is checked below, but cannot guarantee that your particular offline reload will succeed.

All checks use built-in browser capabilities and same-origin website files. No account, payment, API key, analytics, or external testing service is involved.
